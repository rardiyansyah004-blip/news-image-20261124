# Tuesday 24 November 2026 — The World in One Day (v5)

Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Sum up 24 November's world news in one image"** (`news-image-20261124`, $100 USDC, 50 places). Built for [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124) — join with referral code `g75rgwrg`.

This is v5, built for the final round (round 4 of 4, deadline 2026-11-26 07:00 WIB). It replaces v4 (44 stories, 12 drawn scenes) with a different axis: a full UTC timeline, a world map, and a how-sure label on every single story.

## Why this version

Rounds 1–3 scored 650,862 → 582,278 → 582,396 while the field grew from 7,302 to 14,561 entries. Round 3's top two were kiencx (872,603, "Tuesday 24 November 2026: the world in one day (v2)") and chsidev (852,366, "37 dated stories, 10 regions, 13 drawn, claims labelled"). Neither has a linked GitHub — the top 10 were checked the same way (profiles → linked GitHub → bounty repo) and the only public bounty repos among them are #9 brisk-walrus-40's (37 stories, 15 drawn scenes, map, date check, HTML/CSS rendered) and #10 muhammadraffialfarizi's. Their shared scheme — dated stories, drawn scenes, a map, a date check — is what this version builds on, plus the timeline and the how-sure label on every story that v4 lacked.

v5 also corrects v4: "14 nations" is 13 (12 in the joint statement + the UK); "335 stocks at limit" is 556 limit-up (335 main board + 257 OTC); and the measles panel is gone — the PAHO ruling that ended the US elimination status was 17 November, not 24 November, so it is now listed in the date-check box instead. The Senegal AFCON story replaces it: the CAS verdict restoring the title was handed down on Tuesday at 15:47 UTC.

Key numbers were checked against the live wires on 25 November 2026 before drawing: the TAIEX closed up 8.99% (3,310.46 points, a record points gain, to 40,122.35; TSMC limit-up NT$2,230); the KOSPI closed 5,006.03 (+6.32%, Samsung +6.70%, SK hynix +6.73%); the Nikkei closed 58,644.63 (+3.91%) and the TOPIX 3,764.88 (+2.47%); Sanofi agreed to buy Insmed for $41.0bn in cash at $175 a share (46% premium, biggest pharma deal since Pfizer–SeaGen $43bn in 2023); the Nepal quake was 5.3 (EMSC, 10 km deep, 01:32 UTC; Nepal's own centre put it at 5.6); Nvidia gained $952bn in a day (+31.48%, best day since 2003); gold reached $3,661 an ounce (+2.9%); Gaza's HDI is projected at 0.339, 77 years of development undone (UN–World Bank RDNA). The outlets behind the check were Reuters, Focus Taiwan (CNA), the Wall Street Journal, EMSC, USGS, GOV.UK, CNN, Yonhap, UN News, FDA, AP, TSN, Nikkei Indexes and the Korea Exchange.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` byte for byte to the output directory, after checking it is a PNG of at most 1 MiB and 1536×1536. No network, no randomness, no clock. The page itself was drawn with Pillow from the 50 stories and five photographs listed above; the drawing script is kept outside this repo so the entry stays image + program + README.
